Organic Groove Lab

The generative drum engine from the Max device and the plugin, running here with placeholder sounds. Shape a groove, then save it as a MIDI clip for Ableton or copy the settings into the device.

Knobs

Drag a knob up or down (Shift for fine steps), scroll on it, or use the arrow keys. Double-click resets it. Changes are heard within a beat or two.

Kit

Pick a sound per lane: the placeholder synth, an uploaded sample (or drop a file on the lane), or a recording. Start sets where each hit plays from: From start, a Random point, or Mixed (accents from the start, other hits half the time from inside). Length shortens long samples. Samples stay in this browser. Lanes use Drum Rack notes on MIDI channel 1.
